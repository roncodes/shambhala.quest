Shambhala, Inc is a nonprofit dedicated to supporting the restoration and spiritual mission of Khamar Monastery in the Gobi Desert.

About Shambhala, Inc

Shambhala, Inc is a registered U.S. nonprofit (501(c)(3)) committed to the preservation of spiritual and cultural heritage in Mongolia. Our focus is on Khamar Monastery, a historic and sacred site located deep in the Gobi Desert, originally founded by the renowned teacher and poet Danzanravjaa in the 1800s.

Our Mission

  • Support restoration and upkeep of Khamar Monastery.
  • Provide resources to monks and local community members.
  • Facilitate awareness of Mongolia’s spiritual legacy worldwide.
  • Encourage cultural exchange and spiritual learning.
